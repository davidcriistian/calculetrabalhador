# Central Brain — Approval and Publication Manual

## Mandatory roles

- domain-owner;
- legal-reviewer;
- publishing-owner.

## Publication gate

The gate remains closed unless:

- approval artifact is valid and unexpired;
- impact analysis is available;
- execution plan is read-only and approved;
- rollback is defined;
- preview evidence is complete;
- validators pass;
- protected files show no unintended mutation.

## Publication

Publication must be a separate, explicit human-controlled action. The governance layer may prepare and validate, but must not publish automatically.
