# Central Brain — Content Onboarding Manual

## New calculator

Register the calculator in the authoritative tool catalog, define the legal rules it consumes, validate its canonical path and regenerate projections.

## New article

Register the article in the authoritative content catalog, connect it to tools or rules, assign cluster and nucleus, and validate its canonical route.

## New cluster or nucleus

Create the authoritative entry, connect members explicitly and regenerate editorial projections.

## Acceptance criteria

- unique ID;
- valid canonical path;
- explicit dependency;
- no unresolved relation;
- correct cluster and nucleus;
- all validators passing;
- no public mutation during registration.
